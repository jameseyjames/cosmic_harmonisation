# Meeting with Karen Blackmon: harmonisation strategy

Length: 45 min (scale as needed). Goal: understand her approach and decide what we can reuse for COSMIC.

## 1. Intro and context (5 min)
- Our project: harmonise COSMIC cohorts to HRS-HCAP and produce comparable domain and global cognition scores.
- Where we are: proof of concept with English-speaking cohorts (MAS, PATH, EAS, MYHAT), item-level curation, Rmonize.
- What I'd like from today: how her method works, what it needs, and whether it fits.

## 2. Her approach (15 min)
- Can you walk me through the strategy end to end?
- Is the preprint available? (I could only find the AAIC 2025 abstract.)
- How does the categorical method work, and why categorical rather than continuous scores?
- How does the poverty index (counting method, International Brain Health Index) enter? Which indicators and cut-offs?
- Which cohorts and tests did you harmonise, and how many were LMIC?
- How did you link tests across cohorts (anchor items, expert judgement, statistical linking)?

## 3. Validity and limits (10 min)
- How did you check the categories are comparable across cohorts (DIF, sensitivity analyses, external validation)?
- What are the main limitations or things that went wrong?
- How does it handle differences in language, education and culture?
- What do you lose compared with continuous IRT-based scores?

## 4. Fit with COSMIC (10 min)
- Could the categorical approach work alongside or instead of continuous scores for LMIC cohorts?
- Which parts could we reuse (code, definitions, thresholds)? Can she share them?
- Which data would we need (education, income, wealth)? Do COSMIC cohorts have them?
- Is a global cognition category feasible, and what minimum domains did you require?
- Would she review our approach or collaborate?

## 5. Wrap-up (5 min)
- Agree next steps: papers, code or documents to share, a follow-up meeting.
- Who else should I talk to?
